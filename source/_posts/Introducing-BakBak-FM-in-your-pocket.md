Introducing BakBak – Indian FM radio, right in your pocket. Tune into 15+ live Indian radio stations for free, with zero friction.

[<u>App Store link</u>](https://apps.apple.com/in/app/bakbak-fm-in-your-pocket/id6771061849)

{% asset_img screenshots.png %}

I've always loved listening to the radio — the randomness of it, the RJ banter, the songs you didn't know you needed to hear. But every app I tried was either bloated with subscriptions, required an account, or buried the actual radio behind a dozen taps.

So I built BakBak. Open it, tap a station, and you're listening. That's it.

**What's inside:**
- 15+ live Indian radio stations — Mirchi, Big FM, Red FM, IshqFM, AIR FM Rainbow, Vividh Bharati, Radio Indigo and more
- Every genre covered — Bollywood, retro, classical, devotional, news and talk
- Beautiful dark UI with live EQ visualisations
- No account. No subscription. No algorithm. Just radio.
